Move more, naturally

Your body wasn’t made for one position all day.

Long stretches in the same chair can leave your back, shoulders, and energy feeling stuck. A height-adjustable desk makes it easier to break up sitting time and change your posture throughout the day.

A standing desk isn’t a workout or a medical treatment. Think of it as an everyday cue to vary your position and keep gentle movement within reach.

A comfortable starting point

Bring the desk to you.

At either height, let your shoulders relax, keep your elbows near a right angle, and place the top of your screen around eye level. Comfort comes first; fine-tune from there.
