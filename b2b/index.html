08 — Responsibility

The people who make it

Incoming inspection · Plant 2

A column is only as consistent as the people who build it. So the conditions they build it in are part of the specification.

We hold SA8000:2014 for social accountability and ISO 45001:2018 for occupational health and safety. Both are audited by an outside body on a recurring cycle — they are not self-declared statements of intent, and the audit findings are available to customers under NDA.°

SA8000 is the part that usually matters to a European or North American buyer, because it is the standard covering the questions a compliance team is obliged to ask: working hours, wages, freedom of association, health and safety, and the absence of child or forced labour.

Safe work

A managed occupational health and safety system covering guarding, extraction, PPE, incident reporting and annual health checks — audited, with corrective actions tracked to closure.

ISO 45001:2018

Fair work

Working hours, wages, freedom of association and the prohibition of child and forced labour, held to an internationally recognised standard rather than to local minimums.

SA8000:2014

Skilled work

Structured induction and on-the-job training for new operators, and a standing improvement-suggestion scheme through which the people on the line change how the line runs.
